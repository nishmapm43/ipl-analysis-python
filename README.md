# 🏏 IPL Match Performance Analysis (2008–2024)

## Business Question
**What factors predict IPL match outcomes — and what should teams do about it?**

## Dataset
- Source: Kaggle — IPL Complete Dataset 2008–2024
- matches.csv: 900+ matches across 17 seasons
- deliveries.csv: 200,000+ ball-by-ball records

## Tools Used
Python · Pandas · Matplotlib · Seaborn · Jupyter Notebook

## Key Insights

### 1. Toss Advantage is Overrated
Toss winners win only ~50% of matches — barely better than chance.
**Recommendation:** The decision after winning the toss matters more than the toss itself.

### 2. MI and CSK Dominate Across All Seasons
Mumbai Indians leads total wins; CSK leads consistency.
**Recommendation:** Study MI's retention strategy and CSK's leadership stability.

### 3. Chasing Has Become the Winning Strategy
Chasing win rates rose from ~45% (2008) to ~55% (2020+).
**Recommendation:** Build squads with strong death-over batsmen and chase specialists.

### 4. Top Run Scorer and Wicket Taker
Virat Kohli leads all-time IPL runs. Malinga leads wickets.
**Recommendation:** Retain consistent performers over single-season stars.

### 5. Home Venue Advantage is Real
Teams win significantly more at home — Wankhede and Chepauk show strongest advantage.
**Recommendation:** Optimise home scheduling for high-stakes matches.
